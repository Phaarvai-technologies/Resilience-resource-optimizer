Build a detailed, clickable web-app prototype for “Phaarvai Resilience Resource Optimizer,” an open-source AI/GIS and multilingual planning platform for UNICEF Venture Fund Aim 1: optimizing resource allocation for resilient infrastructure, local government readiness, and community preparedness to protect children from climate-health risks.

The prototype should look like a polished early-stage GovTech / climate-tech product demo, suitable for a 2-minute UNICEF pitch video. Use a clean, modern, accessible design with a white background, soft climate colors, map-based visuals, clear cards, and simple iconography. The product should feel trustworthy, practical, and easy for local governments, NGOs, schools, health workers, and community organizations to use.

Core concept:
Phaarvai helps local governments decide where limited climate-resilience resources should go first. It combines climate, health, infrastructure, school/facility, demographic, and community data to generate child-centered vulnerability scores for schools, clinics, anganwadis, neighborhoods, and public assets. It also translates technical risk outputs into simple multilingual guidance for frontline workers and communities.
